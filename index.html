Mystory

iOS 日記 App・免費・無廣告・無內購・尚未上架

Mystory 是一本放在手機裡的手寫日記。每天寫一篇日記，讀完一本書寫書評，看完一部電影寫影評，或回答一道「大哉問」。文章預設私密；你可以選擇公開其中幾篇，讓其他使用者閱讀、收藏、追蹤你。

功能

  • 四種文章：日記、書評、影評、大哉問，支援繁體中文與英文。
  • 書評與影評可從書庫／電影庫挑選作品，資料來源為 Google Books、Open Library 與 TMDB。
  • 書寫成就與連續天數、每日提醒通知。
  • 公開文章的探索頁、關鍵字搜尋、追蹤、收藏、封鎖與檢舉。

開發者

Mystory 由許景評（Steven Hsu）開發與維運；最初的發想與規劃來自吳若安（Joanne Wu）。目前完全免費，沒有廣告，也沒有任何內購或訂閱。

Mystory

iOS diary app · free · no ads · no in-app purchases · not yet released

Mystory is a handwritten-feeling diary that lives on your phone. Write a diary entry each day, a review when you finish a book or a film, or answer one of the "big questions". Entries are private by default; you can choose to publish some of them so other users can read, save and follow you.

Features

  • Four entry types — diary, book review, film review, big question — in Traditional Chinese and English.
  • Book and film reviews pick their title from a catalog backed by Google Books, Open Library and TMDB.
  • Writing achievements, streaks and daily reminder notifications.
  • An explore feed of public entries with keyword search, following, saving, blocking and reporting.

Developer

Mystory is built and operated by Steven Hsu (許景評); the original idea and planning came from Joanne Wu (吳若安). It is free, has no advertising, and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.
